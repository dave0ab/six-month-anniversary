# Six Months of Us 💗 (for Tsiye)

Single-file, mobile-first anniversary site. Just `index.html`, so there's no build step.

## Customize
At the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`:
- `herName`: her nickname (ፅዬ)
- `fromName`: your signature
- `startDate`: the day you got together (drives the live counter)

## Deploy
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Or import this repo on vercel.com (Framework: Other, no build command).
3. Open the link on your phone to test it, then send it to her.
